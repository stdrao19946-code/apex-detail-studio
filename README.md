# Apex Detail Studio

A prototype for a course International Financial Management

**Group members:** Abdullah Bin Waris & Rao Daniyal Ahmed

## Business idea

Apex Detail Studio is a car detailing and ceramic coating workshop in Karachi. We clean, polish and protect cars so the paint looks showroom-new and stays that way. Customers book a slot, bring the car to our workshop and collect it the same day (ceramic coating takes 2 to 3 days). The studio is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 9:00 pm.

## Monthly sales, expenses and profit (steady state)

Expected cars per month: 50 washes, 20 interior cleans, 12 full details, 4 two-year coatings, 1 five-year coating.

| Item | PKR per month |
|---|---|
| **Sales** | **866,000** |
| Materials and chemicals | 206,000 |
| Workshop rent | 80,000 |
| Salaries (lead detailer 55,000; detailer 40,000; helper 28,000; two partners 50,000 each) | 223,000 |
| Electricity, water, gas | 35,000 |
| Marketing | 40,000 |
| Maintenance, internet, other | 30,000 |
| **Total expenses** | **614,000** |
| **Expected profit (sales − expenses)** | **252,000** |

- Profit margin: about 29.1%. Profit is shared equally, about PKR 126,000 per partner per month, on top of each partner’s PKR 50,000 salary.
- Fixed costs: 408,000 per month. Break-even sales: about 535,345 per month (roughly 62% of expected sales).
- Payback of the 1,500,000 investment: about 6 months at full volume, or about 9 months if the first three months run at 40%, 60% and 80% of expected volume.
- Profit is before tax. Equipment depreciation is not included, to keep the model simple.

## International finance angle: dollar risk

About 70% of our materials (coatings, polishes, chemicals) are imported and priced in USD. The prototype assumes a base rate of PKR 276.50 per USD (1 USD = 276.50 PKR), and lets you move it between 250 and 400. If the rupee weakens, material costs rise and profit falls. Our responses are bulk buying of stock, quarterly price reviews and adding local suppliers.
